●

✓ The Three-Pillar Alternative

80% of employees elect our strategy and pay less, with more membership benefits

✓

Layer 1 — Preventive EAP: Covers every employee. Wellness doctor visits, preventive labs up to $4,500, mental health up to $3,000, dental cleanings, vision screening, telehealth, and generic Rx. Up to $9,000 in annual benefits per member.

✓

Layer 2 — Healthcare Cooperative (HCC): A cooperative is a member-owned program where Americans contribute to a shared pool that pays for their healthcare — similar to how Medicare works. Just as working Americans pre-pay into Medicare for future benefits, HCC members contribute to a pool that covers claims today. Everyone funds it. Everyone owns it. No networks, no prior authorizations, no claim denials. Employees see any provider. Under 3% annual increase since 2019. 98.7% renewal rate.

✓

Layer 3 — ICHRA for the 20%: Employees who prefer or require traditional ACA coverage get their own marketplace plan funded by a fixed employer contribution. Their claims cannot affect your group’s rates.

✓

Recruiting and retention advantage: Employees see lower monthly member fees, lower out-of-pocket costs, no prior authorizations, and access to any provider. In a competitive labor market, this is a differentiator — not just a benefit line item.

More choices, more protection — not fewer benefits

With an ICHRA Health Option, every employee gains access to the full ACA marketplace — hundreds of plans from multiple carriers. They choose the plan that covers their doctors, their medications, and their family. The employer contributes a fixed monthly amount to fund it.

Employee chooses their own plan Full ACA marketplace access — their doctors, their network, their medications. Real choice, not a one-size-fits-all group plan.
